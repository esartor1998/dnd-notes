The session starts with Soren using a rabbit to confirm that any creature can use return portals to escape the trials (such as [[Aglaraerose]]).

Our group enters the hut to find a frustrating woman. Doc mentions something about paying her at the carnival for a prophecy. She says we can call her [[Gran Gran]]. [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] solves the riddle by ignoring the rules/instructions. We don't drink the tea.

We determine her to be Baba Yaga.

Soren transforms into [[Zybilna]] using Soren's Identity Cascade. Soren realizes that the [[Zybilna]] travelling with us is not the real one, when [[Zybilna]] provides a spellbook page instead of a hair when asked for the spell component for the illusion.

Soren lays a trap for the dragon to save the children in 400 years on the way past the green dragon [[Aglaraerose]].

Entering the palace, the cauldron is 3 feet across and 80 pounds. It has cats and skulls and eyes on it, and looks like Tasha's Bubbling Cauldron.

[[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] inspects a greatsword that looks really sharp, maybe vorpal. When he prods it, he can tell that it doesn't need strength, but rather wit to wield proficiently. When [[Leroux Whitelaw|Leroux]] inspects it, he determines it is [Snicker-Snack](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/wondrous-items:snicker-snack).

Eric finally gets to take a long rest.

Soren establishes that the deer is actually [[Wrath]] (one of [[Zybilna|Zybilna's]] two guardians with [[Envy]]). We learn nothing concrete from him, other than that everything he said he believes to be true.

After the long rest, we take the cauldron, hint to the dragon that it can escape on our way out, and immediately start searching for the captured children. It takes us 26 minutes to find them. [[Soren]] immediately starts casting Teleportation Circle to the Seelie Court to get the children out of here. We successfully evacuate the children, and then the hag of the past shows up, asks us if we know what stealing the cauldron means, and then we roll initiative.
