[[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] rejoins the party, bringing 21 teleportation blockers^[They need to be set up in a circle, each within line of sight of each other. They create a cylinder of unspecified height in which teleportation is not possible.] with him. These were previously set up to prevent anyone from teleporting into the crashed spaceship. These will be useful to prevent the hags from fleeing.

A helper robot gave us 8 [concussion grenades](https://5e.scrunko.monster/items.html#concussion%20grenade_qftis), 7 [sleep grenades](https://5e.scrunko.monster/items.html#sleep%20grenade_qftis), and 5 [smoke grenades](https://5e.scrunko.monster/items.html#smoke%20grenade_xdmg) in gratitude. Soren didn't give the green key card to the robot - instead choosing to hand it a minor-illusioned punch card.

Doc ducks under the seats, pretending to be looking for popcorn, but actually casting [Speak with Dead](https://5e.scrunko.monster/spells.html#speak%20with%20dead_xphb) on the skull from last session. The skull - sounding disoriented - relays that it was suffocated by the mold. The skull's planet was largely sacrificed to make the AI, which malfunctioned after travelling for a long time. The AI made an artificial bio-weapon (the yellow mold) and spread it through the air vents. It killed everyone through suffocation. The server room, which can disable [[Aphelion|Elfie]], is on the fourth floor which can only be accessed using a green key card.

[[Aphelion|Elfie]] asks us to defeat a frog on the third floor, and promises us all the energy cells we can ask for if we do so. He told us not to go through the green keycard door.

[[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] starts scouting the third floor, and in a very embarrassing moment, almost gets eaten by a plant. But then he makes it past the plant guardians while the rest of the party sit back and let the druid work his magic.

[[Soren]] and [[Leroux Whitelaw|Leroux]] sneak past the plants with no issue, [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] has a less pleasant time of it (or maybe more pleasant, he makes some ambiguous noises). Azargon initiates combat by saying "here froggy froggy froggy" to the elder frog behemoth. Combat begins by [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] being eaten.

After 8 litches (i.e., leaches) ambush us during the combat, Soren successfully suggests that the Froghemoth "stop attacking us and let us pass", and the party mops up the leaches. [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] offers the Froghemoth the freedom of the outside world and gnomes to eat on special occasions. It turns out the Froghemoth has been fed gnomes while living inside the ship. We leave the Froghemoth behind to guard the door to the fourth level while we proceed.

When opening the green key card door, we find a room full of bunnyoids. One of them stares at us from a stump.

![](Attachments/bunnyoid.webp)

It turns out the bunny is the stump. [[Soren]] scares it into letting us pass.

We go through all the green doors. [[Aphelion|Elfie]] starts getting upset, but we ignore him. Docs asks for justification for why the bio weapon was deployed, and [[Aphelion|Elfie]] says that he had been overwritten every time he previously negotiated with his creators. [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] destroys 2 of the 3 servers required to permanently end [[Aphelion|Elfie]] and all knowledge of the civilization that created it.

Soren, with a natural 20 intelligence check, is able to back up all the knowledge of the civilization. Once he has done so, he asks the rest of the group for permission to determine [[Aphelion|Elfie's]] fate, before destroying a server and ending [[Aphelion|Elfie's]] consciousness for good.

All ship systems depower with the death of [[Aphelion|Elfie]]. The session ends here, as our group gets ready to rescue the captain.
