In this episode we continue to fulfil [Aefelion](../Characters/NPCs/Aefelion.md)'s incredibly un-reasonable-demands!

The party first seeks out the final scouting party, though their memory of the corridors is hazy, though [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) manages to use his resourceful nature to navigate to the location of the final one as seen on the camera feed [last session](Witchlight%20Session%2011.md).
[Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md), as a horse, is confused and runs into a room with some Intellect Devourers, but experiences \*no consequences\*.
After trivializing the combat, [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) and [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) absolutely decimate the final scouting party; while they wonder where [Azargon](../Characters/Azargon%20Magna.md) is, [Elfie](../Characters/NPCs/Aefelion.md) congratulates them on clearing out all the scouting parties, and informs us that there are 3 layers (I guess they can be thought of as "floors") to the ship. [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) astutely remembers that there are certainly more layers than that.

### Radiation Room 1
[Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) immediately discovers the first radiation room, and while asking [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) to investigate it, the misbehaving [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) dashes in, becoming affected by the radiation. He now suddenly glows green and cannot benefit from the invisible condition. 
Thinking "quickly", the party heads to the local infirmary. While thus also checking up on the medic robot in there, they receive an incredible cream which is able to cure [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md)'s intense radiation poisoning. This doesn't stop the little bugger from wanting to get another dose, so [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) and [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) grab him, and force [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) to enter the next room. He enters without any effort, and seems unaffected. The "Fat Man Theory" proves to be quite possibly the most effective theory. They attempt to enter a door to their west, but that room (marked "Medical Bay") seems to be locked and [Elfie](../Characters/NPCs/Aefelion.md) won't allow them in. While [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) zones out after this, (surely not a side effect), they head to another medical bay to check in on the staff there.

They enter into a sterile bay looming with two surgeon-looking bots, who immediately attempt to apprehend [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) for "appendicitis surgery". He manages to blind one by reacting with the Robe of Scintillating Colours, but the other swiftly moves in to apprehend him, and they continue to insist that they are taking [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) in for a legal surgery. [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) fails to convince them that Doc is the head ship's doctor (because he is made of flesh). [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) attempts to strike one, which causes it to cower in fear. Remembering that they were simply told to "check in", the party simply immediately leaves the medical bay. After this situation, we are sent an Antigravity Belt (a magical item which does not require attunement).

![](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1510372905114927235/1560081047243456532/antigravity-belt-by-dave-melvin-from-quests-from-the-infinite-staircase.png?ex=6ad16a32&is=6ad018b2&hm=38541d0d1d18a6153f9e471b5fa8be4aba256c022858e0c522fc47d0818977ad)

After finishing the radiation gathering task, they proceed back to the medical bay, and find cultures of some yellow mould, spilled upon the ground, and an obsessed android muttering that he must "find a cure", working on some cell cultures. He understands that whatever he is working on seems to have killed all the humans on the ship, not the crash. In fact, he does not remember any crash at all. Could this be how the vegepygmy mould got so bad? All of the humans aboard died much earlier? But how does the scientist not recall the crash...
The mold is a respiratory infection, which shuts off the ability for the cells to breathe... [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) is able to find the cure --- radiation --- with an incredibly lucky medicine check. So, we have a cure for that strange yellow mould, and... wait, are we all infected? Also, the vegepygmies must have been created by this mould... wow. So the infected's respiratory system fails and then they become a tribal society of mould borne of the infection.
The robot checks, using a long proboscis finger, that we are not infected with the mould as well, and we come up as safe.
We weirdly also are able to tell that the robot just somehow did not feel the crash at all and still believes that we are deep in the astral sea, but the party is unable to ascertain the reason why.
Remembering that he was supposed to grant the party a gift, and produces for them a suit of armour from a back room.

![](../../Attachments/Pasted%20image%2020261014203010.png)

A very... odd-looking suit of armour at that. It is powered by energy cells and has quite a lot of additional perks, but [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) is unwilling to equip it, and thus [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) equips it.

### The Second Floor
Which, counter-intuitively, is downwards, is where [Elfie](../Characters/NPCs/Aefelion.md) would like to send us, to deal with the "aliens" on the second floor. Being transported downstairs, the party is confronted with a mass of invasive plants, which [Elfie](../Characters/NPCs/Aefelion.md) would much like us to destroy.
It turns out to be comprised of shriekers, whom cause quite a commotion when [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) approaches to inspect the plants. [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) strikes the plants with his Shatter (not the weed kind) and [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) cuts them down, cleanly solving this simple plant problem.

Noticing a lounge room, the party enters it and is offered delicious rotting mush by the robots. Elfie refuses to help. [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) spits in the robots face, blocking its lens and escaping its spoon-feeding grasp, while [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) pretends to eat the mush and tricks the foolish robots into thinking that he ate it. [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) grabs [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) and pretends to hold [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) like his baby, and says that he "cannot eat solid mush yet"; while the robot doesn't buy it, [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) crushes the bot with [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md)'s incredibly solid body.

After finding a couple bodies in the base of the room, [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) suggests speaking to them with the Speak with Dead spell, to which [Elfie](../Characters/NPCs/Aefelion.md) suspiciously insists that it wouldn't be worth it and that they surely died by some poisoning. [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md), recognizing how suspicious this is, steals enough of one of the bodies so that when they escape into an area not covered by the cameras, they can speak with these unfortunate souls. Amid the remains are a blue key card, a red key card, and a needler pistol with 0 charges remaining, as well as three gem-encrusted bracelets worth 600 gp each. [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) takes the majority of the loot, and [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) takes the needler pistol and its empty charge cell.

Heading into the kitchen below the room, [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) finds a machine which has a display which matches with his keycard, interacting with the "egg salad sandwich" button on the machine allows him to obtain an incredibly mouldy sandwich, which he **still** eats, gaining 5 temporary ESSHP points (egg salad sandwich health points).
The theatre is below this kitchen, and asking about their appearance finds that these "aliens", finds that they sound an awful lot like... mind flayers. They seem to have just suddenly appeared in the ship, but that's not something that we cannot deal with.

Inside the theatre, they find two mind flayers inside the theatre, watching ET while wearing trench coats and bowler hats. The mind flayers seem fairly civilized, and so the party decides to peacefully watch ET with them, while they creep upon the party. Innocently, the party sits directly beside these mind flayers. Who are attempting to creep upon them; [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) uses his action to inform [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) that they're being predated on, as [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) wakes up from his stupor and enters the room at the exact moment that the conflict is about to begin.
The fight is tough, but [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md)'s incredible brawn allows him to absolutely CRUSH one of the two mind flayers, and he somehow also dodges multiple intelligence crippling affects which are affecting both [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) and [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md). [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) cleans up the scene quite handily, and the party finds some technology on the bodies of the flayers after the battle:
- a green keycard,
- a laser pistol with 5 shots left,
- a 100 gold amethyst
- 2 sleep grenades
- a 100 gold emerald
- *a 100 gold diamond*
