The party first seeks out the final scouting party, though their memory of the corridors is hazy, though [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) manages to use his resourceful nature to navigate to the location of the final one as seen on the camera feed [last session](Witchlight%20Session%2011.md).
[Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md), as a horse, is confused and runs into a room with some Intellect Devourers, but experiences \*no consequences\*.
After trivializing the combat, [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) and [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) absolutely decimate the final scouting party; while they wonder where [Azargon](../Characters/Azargon%20Magna.md) is, [Elfie](../Characters/NPCs/Aefelion.md) congratulates them on clearing out all the scouting parties, and informs us that there are 3 layers (I guess they can be thought of as "floors") to the ship. [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) astutely remembers that there are certainly more layers than that.

### Radiation Room 1
[Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) immediately discovers the first radiation room, and while asking [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) to investigate it, the misbehaving [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) dashes in, becoming affected by the radiation. He now suddenly glows green and cannot benefit from the invisible condition. 
Thinking "quickly", the party heads to the local infirmary. While thus also checking up on the medic robot in there, they receive an incredible cream which is able to cure [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md)'s intense radiation poisoning. This doesn't stop the little bugger from wanting to get another dose, so [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) and [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) grab him, and force [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) to enter the next room. He enters without any effort, and seems unaffected. The "Fat Man Theory" proves to be quite possibly the most effective theory. They attempt to enter a door to their west, but that room (marked "Medical Bay") seems to be locked and [Elfie](../Characters/NPCs/Aefelion.md) won't allow them in. While [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) zones out after this, (surely not a side effect), they head to another medical bay to check in on the staff there.

They enter into a sterile bay looming with two surgeon-looking bots, who immediately attempt to apprehend [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) for "appendicitis surgery". He manages to blind one by reacting with the Robe of Scintillating Colours, but the other swiftly moves in to apprehend him, and they continue to insist that they are taking [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) in for a legal surgery. [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) fails to convince them that Doc is the head ship's doctor (because he is made of flesh). [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md) attempts to strike one, which causes it to cower in fear. Remembering that they were simply told to "check in", the party simply immediately leaves the medical bay. After this situation, we are sent an Antigravity Belt (a magical item which does not require attunement).
