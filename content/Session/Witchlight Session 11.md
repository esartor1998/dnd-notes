### If there ever were a sign

While the party is a bit lost, debating what to do next, given they appear to have just run out of rope to follow in their quest (their [[Skibidi Toilet|hag trail]] escaped [[Witchlight Session 10|last session]] with [[Iggwilv's Flametongue Sword|the sword they need]], and there appears to be no way to find her), a meteor-thing falls from the sky, crash landing with a tremendous boom on the other side of Prismeer. Thinking, "well, if there were ever a sign, something falling from the sky would be it" (as [[Soren]] so eloquently put it), the party gets ready to mount on their goat [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] as normal and sets off to investigate. But, before they do, [[Zybilna]] chimes in... hopping out of her Pokeball, she provides a stern warning: that meteor is the source of the Colossus that we faced in the Present domain, and her past self (an incredibly powerful wizard, and in the Past, the archfey of Prismeer) will be there, investigating the intrusion into her domain.
