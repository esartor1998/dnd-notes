### If there ever were a sign

While the party is a bit lost, debating what to do next, given they appear to have just run out of rope to follow in their quest (their [[Skibidi Toilet|hag trail]] escaped [[Witchlight Session 10|last session]] with [[Iggwilv's Flametongue Sword|the sword they need]], and there appears to be no way to find her), a meteor-thing falls from the sky, crash landing with a tremendous boom on the other side of Prismeer. Thinking, "well, if there were ever a sign, something falling from the sky would be it" (as [[Soren]] so eloquently put it), the party gets ready to mount on their goat [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] as normal and sets off to investigate. But, before they do, [[Zybilna]] chimes in... hopping out of her Pokeball, she provides a stern warning: that meteor is the source of the Colossus that we faced in the Present domain, and her past self (an incredibly powerful wizard, and in the Past, the archfey of Prismeer) will be there, investigating the intrusion into her domain. 
Alongside her will be her (demonic, strangely enough) minions, which nobody really comments on.

The object, she says, is a spaceship, with a shattered hatch in the roof into which we could subtlely climb to avoid her might. Additionally, if we manage to dispatch all 3 of the scouting parties she sends into the ship (each comprised of an invisible quaasit, some weird hell-birds/bugs, and a "bulgugna"), her past self will surmise that the location is too dangerous to venture into and retreat.
Within said spaceship are various creatures, from mind flayers to humanoids, including one named [Elfie](../Characters/NPCs/Aefelion.md) whom she says is "comprised of wires and with no body" on a hidden floor of the ship and whom she deceitfully enslaved into becoming the colossus which faced the party the other day (or, the same day? Time travel sucks, man). Again not questioning her Machiavellian evil nature, the party agrees with her that entering into the ship and making a similar deal with Elfie before she can does indeed seem to be the best course of action, sneaking up to the craft and entering without much trouble.

### Entering into the ship

After entering the ship through the skylight, the party finds themselves inside of a golden-age-sci-fi cafeteria, obviously thrown into chaos by the crash and littered with humanoid bodies. [Azargon](../Characters/Azargon%20Magna.md) becomes frightened by the bodies, schizophrenically believing that they will rise and attack him, and thus eviscerates every single one in the room before acting all coy about it like 2 minutes later, though the party did in fact notice and are rather concerned for him...
Pushing onwards though, the [Leroux](../Characters/Leroux%20Whitelaw.md), [Azargon](../Characters/Azargon%20Magna.md) and [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) also encounter a strange device with a slot for a keycard and a keypad which purports to contains egg salad sandwiches in the other room (which looks to [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) to be a kitchen of sorts). Thinking quickly, [Azargon](../Characters/Azargon%20Magna.md) smashes the device, causing multiple security grenades to fall out from it and which gravely injures nearby party members.

After this situation unfolds, the party regroups and heals, and [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md) and [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) encounter a robot-like creature on the ground on a room to the northeast of the cafeteria. He is begging for help, saying he needs assistance getting up, but when [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) attempts to render such aid, the robot says "haha! I've tricked you!" and attempts to shoot him with a paralysis gun, before missing several times and having it ripped from his hands like a misbehaving child by the two adventurers. While they interrogate the robot as to its motivations, it glitches out and beams us a direct transmission introducing them to [Elfie](../Characters/NPCs/Aefelion.md). He requests that the party come to meet him, and sends an escort droid to bring them to his headquarters near to a currently-closed clinic, located somewhere east of the west tube, for their rendezvous (as he cannot move).

### Meeting with [Aefelion](../Characters/NPCs/Aefelion.md)
Truly this must be a foolish being of sorts, as he really believes that [Azargon](../Characters/Azargon%20Magna.md) is the leader of the party, basing his reasoning on our words and on his alphabetic prominence. Seeing that the party has arms and legs and is capable of following commands, he makes a deal with them. He may or may not have said he would benefit them, but in exchange for completing his tedium, the party will gain... something. His tasks are as follows:
1. Collect "samples" from 3 rooms with "radiation" (still not sure what that is, just that it's dangerous)
2. Defeat some intrusive Vegepygmies that are no longer welcome
3. Deal with the other invaders, the scouting parties of [Zybilna](../Characters/NPCs/Zybilna.md)
4. Note all of the glitchy-ass androids on the first layer
Sounds simple enough, right?
Obviously, given the pressing nature of #3, the party decides the actively pursue the scouting parties in the ship. Each combat is fairly trivial, so I won't detail them in too much... detail.

### Trivializing Combat

Scouting party 1 is located and defeated just a short walk away from the headquarters.

Trying to avoid the vegepygmies after the first encounter with a scouting party, the party naturally stumbles into their food supply (they appear to be a much more pervasive force than [Elfie](../Characters/NPCs/Aefelion.md) implied, seeming to take up a very large portion of the ship). Their food supply, as it happens, tends to scream! Such a racket naturally alerts some guard vegepygmies, who come out to investigate and encounter the party's top negotiator, [Azargon](../Characters/Azargon%20Magna.md), deeply inspecting some of the shriekers. Accusing him of being a Southern Vegepygmy, he denies it. Using his expert negotiation power, he informs them that he is out "exterminating pests" and that he doesn't wish to kill them "yet". Obviously alarmed, the vegepygmies become riled up until [Soren](../Characters/Soren.md) defuses the situation, and they leave saying they'll tell the others about our chat.

Encountering the second scouting party shortly after this, the party fumbles the defeat of one of the mosquito-things, and it begins to fly away at an incredible rate! [Doc](../Characters/Fionn%20Drake.md), using his wild shape for the first real time this campaign, transforms into an astrological horse and chases the freak-ass thing down, grappling it for [Azargon](../Characters/Azargon%20Magna.md) to finish off.
