The party finds themselves, after leaving the previous puzzle, in a supersized Dragonchess-style game (though the movement seems to be free-roam). Also, [[Oakley Tree|Oakley]] has shown up to help us win this game (he is big Dragonchess fan himself, actually). Being such a big fan and game expert, [[Oakley Tree|Oakley]] instantly chooses to be Rook; [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] and [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] fight over being king before deciding that [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] should take king and [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] queen. Soren recognizes the sightlines made by the board (there is a checkerboard of complete darkness, allowing only diagonal sightlines), reckkons that being a Bishop would be premium. Finally, having only melee options, [[Leroux Whitelaw|Leroux]] decides to do a pawn.

To initiate combat, the wildfire spirit teleports the team's queen and king to the center of the board. Then, the opponent takes a legendary action, the effect of which we cannot know since the whole board is vision-blocked from us. Then, [[Soren]] the bishop warps to a similar area, blessing us as he passes, but also moves his full movement across the entire board, spotting the entire other party and identifying them better than [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] ever could have as a beetle. There are a lot of legendary actions and shit happening, and the enemy is shapeshifted into a bat who can see us all easily and target those (that it can legally target). Very interesting. Our king [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] runs directly at the enemy team, and attempts to kill them all in one go.
