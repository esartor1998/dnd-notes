### The Big Fight
The party takes it upon themselves to save the civilians in the village which is currently being burned, having reasoned that the better option in regards to the sword is to simply camp the [[World Tree]] after dealing with the situation in the village, given what the Legend Lore cast by [[Leroux Whitelaw|Leroux]] informed them.

During the scuffle, [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] created a safe-house by allowing the villagers to Create Water above one of the burning home, allowing him and [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] to begin to move the civilians sheltering in their burning homes there after repelling the skeletons. [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] turns the undead burning skeletons in his vicinity multiple times while leading the civilians to safety, inadvertently driving a few into the forest which they then begin to burn. [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] finally finishes stocking the safe-house when suddenly... a T-Rex appears from the woods? After some bashing, we discover that this must be someone---or some*thing*---polymorphed, and resolve to take it down a size (or three).
The T-rex, not appreciating this, sprints directly to Doc and bites him, pulling him into the air... seeing that [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] is incredibly hurt and in quite a precarious position, [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] decides to perform his signature "fight fire with fire" move which he was just talking about two seconds before, and Polymorphs [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] into another T-rex, instantly granting him a large pool of temporary hit points and freeing him from being grappled and restrained in the T-rex's mouth.
