Flying away, from the gnome beasties that were serving the hag and trying to stash away the [[Iggwilv's Frostbrand Blade]], we see a cloud of smoke gathering above the more bustling town of [[Thistle]], but the party knows their last chance to re-obtain the [[Iggwillv's Flametongue Sword]] is dwindling, as they see the present-version of the sword flickering out of reality on [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]]'s back. 

Pondering the current (thus past?) status of the sword, [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] implores [[Leroux Whitelaw|Leroux]] cast Legend Lore on it; they find that it was once owned by Iggwilv, and held in a vault, but then moved to a cave under a lake... but then, he changes his mind, it actually has been taken to destroy the [[World Tree]].
Looking downwards, the party also sees that a large party of burning skeletons seems to be razing the village below, which they recognize as [[Thistle]] from what they had seen in the Present.
### The Big Fight
The party takes it upon themselves to save the civilians in the village which is currently being burned, having reasoned that the better option in regards to the sword is to simply camp the [[World Tree]] after dealing with the situation in the village, given what the Legend Lore cast by [[Leroux Whitelaw|Leroux]] informed them.

During the scuffle, [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] created a safe-house by allowing the villagers to Create Water above one of the burning home, allowing him and [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] to begin to move the civilians sheltering in their burning homes there after repelling the skeletons. [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] turns the undead burning skeletons in his vicinity multiple times while leading the civilians to safety, inadvertently driving a few into the forest which they then begin to burn. [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] finally finishes stocking the safe-house when suddenly... a T-Rex appears from the woods? After some bashing, we discover that this must be someone---or some*thing*---polymorphed, and resolve to take it down a size (or three).
The T-rex, not appreciating this, sprints directly to Doc and bites him, pulling him into the air... seeing that [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] is incredibly hurt and in quite a precarious position, [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] decides to perform his signature "fight fire with fire" move which he was just talking about two seconds before, and Polymorphs [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] into another T-rex, instantly granting him a large pool of temporary hit points and freeing him from being grappled and restrained in the T-rex's mouth.
We then combo the hag, who futilely attempts to chain lightning and induce madness in the innocent civilians of the village, but she has no chance. Chomped by T-rex [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] and then eviscerated by a furious [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]], she is killed within minutes. She does manage to down a single villager, but the quick-thinking party stabilizes then and then gets to putting out the fire that continues to rage through this market district. T-Rex [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] uses his large 50-gallon mouth to put out the fire on some houses near the river, and [[Azargon Magna|Azargon]] uses Control Flames to stop another house from destruction. Then, circle-casting with the spellcasting villagers, the party is able to cause a torrential downpour to save all the rest of the buildings at the last minute!
Azargon then again "fights fire with fire" by creating a controlled burn to stop the forest fire from spreading beyond the range initially started by the skeletons.
