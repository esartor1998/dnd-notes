### Meeting the Hag
- The hag appears to know [[Fionn Drake|Doc]], and says that he foiled her underling's plans earlier; likely referring to the weird freak he scared off earlier that day.
- Seeing [[Leroux Whitelaw|Leroux]], she seems to be aware of him as well...
	- "Back fiend!" says the valiant [[Leroux Whitelaw|Leroux]], who swings and grazes the hag, despite her attempt at [[divination magic]]s to avoid it
- After a short battle of combat, and freshly full of rage, [[Azargon]] makes short work of the Hag, who appears to be a simulacrum of some powerful creature as snow begins to drip from it...
- [[Leroux Whitelaw|Leroux]] is able to close the gap (after hearing that he is pregnant, and thus being full of rage) and finish the simulacrum off, with the assistance of [[Azargon]]'s new ability to cast a spell as an opportunity attack!

Upon her death, the hag's simulacrum, she invites [[Azargon]] and [[Leroux Whitelaw|Leroux]] to the Crystal Palace!

- A group of wraiths and their army of spectres arrived as well, an army of spectres of children that [[Azargon]] was tricked into massacring...
	- Of course they are made short work of them.

[[Zybilna]], still as a deer, arrives just after the final wraith is wiped, wondering what happened. She implores [[Azargon]] not to cloud his mind with guilt and ensures us that we can undo what the hags have done... She also tells us that the portal to Prismeer has just opened, and that we need to act fast!
[[Azargon]] attempts to cast "Locate Object" on the hag's simulacrum's remaining cloth, but it fails... Does this mean that she is watching?

[[Azargon]] reveals that he was tricked into killing the children by having the children be illusioned into casting a ritual...

> If you want to revive these children, surrender at the swamp in Prismeer...

[[Fionn Drake|Doc]] agrees to agree on a secret handshake with [[Zybilna]]:
left horn, right horn, both horns up and down and then a spin.

Arch-Hags have one weakness: an anti-phylactery; the item that they *hate* most is their source of immortality. [[Zybilna]] says that she does not want to say **why** the hags hate this item, but the hags despise a specific cauldron, stored within the [[Palace of Heart's Desires]], in the pink lake surrounding [[Zybilna]]'s castle. 
[[Azargon]] manages to convince [[Zybilna]] to say that the cauldron was created by a certain person and their mother; this certain person is whom they were jealous of the most, and the other is one whom they sought the attention of. 
These hags once "assisted" [[Zybilna]] in the creation of her realm, pretending to want to change their ways before betraying her; she trusted them as she was once a bad person who changed as well. She wanted to provide them a second chance.

[[Azargon]] goes to see the parent of a kidnapped child, finding out about a [[Summer Eladrin Baby]], Eileen, who was taken by the hags; he is able to scry and find the child in a separate, more-nicely-treated group of child captives who are being kept in a crystal-walled area, likely in the [[Crystal Palace]]...
[[Azargon]] and [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] went to cure the guards whom had been locked up, though it didn't quite go so well, as [[Fionn Drake|Doc]] rolled pretty low on his Dispel Magics! The dispelled guards calmly explain to the ones who are still under the influence that they need to cage themselves, and they comply, as long as they are not to be caged overnight.
