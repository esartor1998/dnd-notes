Azargon, the king of tardiness, has reappeared in a flash of lightning along with the hags. He appears to have been crying, but he won't explain why...
### Meeting the Hag
- The hag appears to know Doc, and says that he foiled her underling's plans earlier; likely referring to the weird freak he scared off earlier that day.
- Seeing Leroux, she seems to be aware of him as well...
	- "Back fiend!" says the valiant Leroux, who swings and grazes the hag, despite her attempt at [[divination magic]]s to avoid it
- After a short battle of combat, and freshly full of rage, Azargon makes short work of the Hag, who appears to be a simulacrum of some powerful creature as snow begins to drip from it...
- Leroux is able to close the gap and finish the simulacrum off, with the assistance of Azargon's new ability to cast a spell as an opportunity attack!
