The central computer upon the ship that crashed in Past Prismeer; a being of wires and an artificial intelligence. Coming from a planet which was exhausted all of its resources on a mission to colonize another habitable planet with the humanoids aboard his ship.

He cannot directly control things upon the ship, but he uses adventurers (or, in the Present Prismeer before modification, he tasked [Zybilna](Zybilna.md)) in doing his minor chores to bring order back to the ship after its crash-landing.

His outward image in the past is a big large monitor with a large central blue eye.
He believes that [Azargon](../Azargon%20Magna.md) is the highest ranked party member, our "leader" (because his name comes first alphabetically).

Has issues with the Vegepygmies and mold aboard his ship, with radiation rooms, and with past-[Zybilna](Zybilna.md)'s scouting parties intruding.

Malfunctioned and attempted to kill all living creatures on the ship using a bio weapon of its own design.
