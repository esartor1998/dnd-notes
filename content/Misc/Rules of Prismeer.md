### Full Rules:
### 1. Rule of Hospitality

> **When a friend, an enemy, or a stranger enters your home, you are expected to be gracious and accommodating until they prove themselves undeserving.**

This means:
- Hosts offer food, shelter, and protection.
- Guests are expected to behave respectfully.
- Attacking or betraying a welcomed guest is a serious breach of etiquette.
- Likewise, abusing someone's hospitality can cause you to lose its protection.

### 2. Rule of Ownership

> **You must not steal from anyone. Taking something without its owner's permission is an unforgivable breach of etiquette.**

This applies to:
- Money and possessions.
- Magical items.
- Even seemingly abandoned property may still be considered owned.

Many fey take this rule very seriously, though some twist it. For example, a hag might argue that because she "owns" a swamp, everything in it—including your backpack—belongs to her.

### 3. Rule of Reciprocity

> **If someone gives you a gift, you are obliged to return something of comparable value, whether immediately or later.**

This doesn't have to be another object:
- A favour.
- A promise.
- A service.
- Valuable information.

This rule explains why fey bargains are so important. Even accepting a small gift may create an obligation that clever fey can exploit.
