### Cal Arc Ideas:
- Even dead, Cal can be one of Fionn's most interesting relationships, precisely *because* Fionn never got to resolve it.
- Cal didn't steal Fionn out of pure altruism or pure agenda. He did it on **impulse**: saw a child born into something monstrous, grabbed him, and ran. And then he spent the rest of his life paying for a decision he made in a single moment. He was harsh not because he was cruel but because he was _terrified_, and he didn't have the tools to parent a child he'd essentially condemned himself to protect. His resentment was never really at Fionn. It was at himself.
- The arc — explored through flashbacks, Feywild dream-visits, letters or objects Elinda kept, or even a time-displaced encounter in Prismeer — could be Fionn slowly realizing this. Maybe there's a letter Cal left with Elinda "in case he never came back." Maybe the simulacrum Elinda holds one of Cal's memories. The emotional beat isn't "I forgive my abusive dad." It's something harder: _"He was trying, in the only way he knew how, and it wasn't enough, and I wish I could have told him that I understand now"._ Cal can grow in Fionn's understanding of him even if not in life.
